Power BI DAX measures
======================

Create a table called Sales by importing data/sales_data.csv. Ensure OrderDate is Date,
Revenue/Cost/Profit/UnitPrice are Decimal Number, Quantity is Whole Number and DiscountPct
is Decimal Number.

Total Revenue = SUM(Sales[Revenue])

Total Profit = SUM(Sales[Profit])

Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[OrderID])

Units Sold = SUM(Sales[Quantity])

Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)

Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Orders], 0)

Average Discount % = AVERAGE(Sales[DiscountPct])

Revenue LY =
CALCULATE([Total Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Calendar'[Date]))

Revenue YoY % =
DIVIDE([Total Revenue] - [Revenue LY], [Revenue LY], 0)

Calendar table (Modeling > New table):
Calendar = CALENDAR(MIN(Sales[OrderDate]), MAX(Sales[OrderDate]))

Then add calculated columns to Calendar:
Year = YEAR('Calendar'[Date])
Month = FORMAT('Calendar'[Date], "MMM")
Month Number = MONTH('Calendar'[Date])
Year-Month = FORMAT('Calendar'[Date], "YYYY-MM")

Sort Calendar[Month] by Calendar[Month Number], then mark Calendar as a date table
(Modeling > Mark as date table). Create a one-to-many relationship from Calendar[Date]
to Sales[OrderDate].
